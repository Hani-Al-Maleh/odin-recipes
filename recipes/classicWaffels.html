Classic Waffles

Waffles with ice cream and banana

Description

A freshly prepared waffle sits at the center of a minimalist scene, served on a smooth white plate that contrasts beautifully with the rich textures and colors of its toppings. The waffle is golden and crisp, topped with a generous scoop of creamy vanilla ice cream that slowly melts into its warm grid. Sliced bananas and ripe strawberries are artfully arranged on top and around the waffle, adding natural sweetness and vibrant pops of yellow and red. A drizzle of glossy chocolate sauce weaves through the fruit and ice cream, bringing depth and indulgence to the presentation.

The plate rests on an elegant marble surface, whose subtle veining enhances the refined, modern feel of the image. The clean background and neutral tones allow the dessert to remain the focal point, making the scene feel both luxurious and inviting. The combination of warm waffle, cool ice cream, fresh fruit, and rich chocolate creates a sense of balance and comfort, capturing a moment that feels perfect for a relaxed brunch or a decadent dessert break.

Ingredients

  • 1 3/4 cups all-purpose flour
  • 3/4 teaspoon baking soda
  • 2 teaspoons white sugar
  • 3/4 teaspoon salt
  • 3 eggs
  • 1 cup heavy cream
  1. Preheat a Belgium waffle iron. Whisk together the flour, baking soda, baking powder, white sugar, and salt in a bowl; set aside.
  2. Whisk together the eggs, 1 1/2 teaspoons vanilla extract, and milk in a bowl. Stir in the melted butter and flour mixture until a slightly lumpy batter forms. Cook the waffles in the preheated iron until steam stops coming out of the seam, about 2 minutes.
  3. Meanwhile, melt 1/4 cup of butter in a saucepan over medium heat. Stir in the brown sugar, rum extract, 2 teaspoons vanilla extract, and cinnamon. Bring to a simmer, the stir in the pecans and continue simmering for 1 minute. Stir in the pancake syrup and bananas, continue cooking until the bananas soften, about 4 minutes.
  4. Beat the heavy cream, 1/4 teaspoon of vanilla and confectioners' sugar with an electric mixer in a medium bowl until firm peaks form.
  5. Once waffles are done, spoon bananas Foster sauce over waffle and top with a dollop of whip cream.
